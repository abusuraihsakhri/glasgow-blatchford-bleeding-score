Clinical context

GBS 0–1: ACG and ESGE guidelines support consideration of outpatient management with follow-up in appropriately assessed, very-low-risk patients.

GBS above 1: The score does not independently mandate admission, intensive care, emergency endoscopy or a particular mortality probability.

Safety: Do not use this tool to delay resuscitation, investigation, or treatment in unstable patients. Clinical judgment and local protocols take precedence.
